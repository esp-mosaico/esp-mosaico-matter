ESP-Mosaico Matter firmware

Download a merged firmware image for ESP-Mosaico (ESP32-S31, 16 MB flash and PSRAM), then use the Mosaico firmware flasher to install it.

Note: The bridge firmware ships with a test Device Attestation Certificate (DAC) and Certification Declaration (CD). Your Matter ecosystem may display a warning that the device is uncertified during commissioning.

Flash your device

  1. Download the .bin for your application above.
  2. Open the Mosaico firmware flasher in desktop Chrome, Edge, or another browser with Web Serial support.
  3. Power off the device and connect its Type-C port with a USB data cable. Hold BOOT, briefly press POWER, then release BOOT.
  4. Select Custom Firmware, choose your downloaded file, select Connect Device, and choose the board's serial port.
  5. Select Start Flashing and keep the cable connected until it finishes. If needed, power the device off and on again after flashing.

Flashing may erase saved data. The merged image is written from address 0x0; you do not need to choose partition offsets.

After flashing, use the on-device setup to connect to Wi-Fi. Open the IP address shown in Settings in a browser to access the Web UI.

For commissioning, agent setup, and accessories, see the Matter hub guide or Matter bridge guide.
